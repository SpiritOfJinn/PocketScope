Pocket Scope Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026

Pocket Scope is a personal budgeting app for iPhone. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and what control you have over it. In short: your financial data is used only to show it back to you. We do not sell it, rent it, or use it for advertising.

Information we collect

  • Bank account information you choose to connect. When you connect a bank, you sign in on a secure screen provided by Plaid Inc. Pocket Scope never sees or stores your bank username or password. Plaid gives us read-only access to the accounts you select: account names, the last few digits of account numbers, account types, balances, and transaction history (dates, amounts, merchant names, and categories).
  • Information you add in the app, such as budgets, category changes, merchant rules, and split transactions.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers.

How we use it

  • To show your balances, spending, budgets, and upcoming bills to you in the app.
  • To keep your accounts up to date when you sync.
  • We do not use your financial data for advertising, and we do not sell, rent, or trade it to anyone.
  • Pocket Scope can only read your data. It cannot move money, make payments, or change anything at your bank.

Who we share it with

We share information only with Plaid Inc., which connects Pocket Scope to your bank, and with the hosting provider that runs our servers, only as needed to operate the app. By connecting a bank, you also agree to Plaid's End User Privacy Policy, which describes how Plaid handles your data. We may also disclose information if required by law.

How we protect it

  • The credential that lets us read your bank data is encrypted before it is stored.
  • Data saved on your iPhone uses Apple's built-in file protection.
  • Access to our systems is limited to the app's developer.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

  • We keep data for a connected bank only while that bank stays connected.
  • Disconnect a bank in the app (Settings, then Bank Connections, then Disconnect). This immediately deletes that bank's accounts and transactions from our servers and tells Plaid to end the connection.
  • Delete everything: email us at the address below and we will delete all of your data within 30 days.
  • Deleting the app removes the copy stored on your iPhone.

Your choices

You decide which banks and accounts to connect, and you can disconnect them at any time. You can ask us for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion by emailing us.

Children

Pocket Scope is not intended for anyone under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
